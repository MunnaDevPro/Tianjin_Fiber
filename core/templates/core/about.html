Who We Are

A Legacy of Strength

Founded in the industrial heart of Tianjin, our company began with a simple mission: to manufacture the most reliable natural fiber ropes in the market. Over the years, we have evolved from a local supplier to a global manufacturing powerhouse.

Today, Tianjin Fiber Net Hemp Rope Manufacturing Co., Ltd. stands as a testament to uncompromising quality. We blend traditional rope-making techniques with modern material science.

Milestones

Our Journey

1995

Foundation

Established first manufacturing facility in Tianjin, focusing purely on natural hemp ropes for local marine industries.

2005

Synthetic Integration

Expanded production lines to include high-strength nylon and polyester netting, catering to construction and safety sectors.

2018

Global Expansion

Opened new automated facility and established international logistics network, exporting to over 50 countries worldwide.
